# Rossmann Store 1

This is a daily sales series. ADF p ≈ 0.0226 rejects a unit root at 5%, and trend-KPSS p ≥ 0.10 does not reject trend stationarity. Results are compatible with stationarity around a trend under these specifications. The spectral peak at L ≈ 365.2 days suggests a possible annual cycle, but repeated annual ACF peaks and multiple cycles are needed to confirm it.
