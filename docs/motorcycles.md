# Motorcycles in Nuevo León

The series has 17 annual observations (2000–2016), increasing from 9,459 to 59,897 overall. ADF p ≈ 0.9986 does not reject a unit root; trend-KPSS p ≥ 0.10 does not reject trend stationarity. These results do not settle the stationarity question. The spectral peak at L = 17 years spans the entire sample and cannot establish repeated seasonality.
